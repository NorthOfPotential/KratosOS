# Approved SUID/SGID binaries and file-capability holders for the KratosOS image

# config/hooks/live/0100-kratos.hook.chroot): exim4 (no MTA), and the setuid
# bit on pppd, ntfs-3g, spice-client-glib-usb-acl-helper, chfn, chsh and
# ssh-keysign.
#
